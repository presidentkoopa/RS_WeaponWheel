# RS_WeaponWheel

Reach out, and your whole arsenal is just *there* — cards hanging in the air
around your hand like you've always got an armory on you. Point and pull the
trigger, or just grab one out of the air. No menu. No lowering your gun to
dig through a list. No waiting for an animation to catch up to your hand.

![The wheel in action](media/demo.gif)
*(sorry the capture quality is rough — VR footage always is)*

That's the whole trick, and it's why it feels so good: open it, point or
reach, take it, done. The ring never shuffles on you — slot 4 is slot 4 on
your first map and your fiftieth, whether you own anything there or not —
so your arm learns it like muscle memory and you stop looking down at your
own hand. Rest on a crowded slot and it fans open like a card trick. Grab a
gun that's run dry and you feel it before you ever pull the trigger.

It also just looks *cool*. Two completely different ways to draw the cards
ship with it: crisp glowing plates that could belong on a starship console,
or painted faces with the art bleeding off the edges like real gear you'd
actually own. Neither is the "correct" one — try both and pick your fantasy.
Placement, size, sound, rumble, all of it lives in a normal options menu;
you will never need to touch a console command.

One catch: this needs a specific VR-flavored engine build, not stock
GZDoom. Setup, keybinds, and every option explained are in
[SETUP.md](SETUP.md); the engineering rationale is in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).
